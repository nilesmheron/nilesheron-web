Niles Heron

Senior Director of Tech-Based Entrepreneurship, TechTown Detroit

bio

A proud product of the Eastside, Niles Heron is the Senior Director of Tech-Based Entrepreneurship at TechTown Detroit. There he leads Venture 313, the Mobility Accelerator Innovation Network (MAIN), Gateway, and TechTown's Core Programs, which together represent more than $30 million in philanthropic commitment to tech-based entrepreneurial development in Southeast Michigan. The work supports local entrepreneurs as they ideate, build products, gain traction, and scale, with programs that range from early founder education and product development to capital strategy and scaling tech businesses.

Niles joined TechTown in late 2024 after nearly a decade as an entrepreneur, advisor, and investor in fintech, environmental science, entertainment, SaaS, and sports. He spent most of that decade as a co-founder and early partner in Loaded and its sister brand Open World, two of the leading management and brand consulting firms in gaming. Over seven years he helped guide Loaded, a management and analytics firm in gaming and livestreaming, to $30 million in equity investment and close to $500 million in gross revenues. His work there covered creator management, content development, partnerships, and campaign strategy for consumer, tech, product, and gaming brands. Under his leadership, Loaded architected and fulfilled creator and brand partnerships with Adidas, Epic Games, Activision, Spotify, P&G, Twitch, YouTube, Red Bull, Pepsi, Chipotle, Postmates, and more.

Niles continues to moonlight in livestreaming and entertainment management and strategic consultation. That includes content and event development for Revolt, 3BD, and DreamCon, and strategic marketing consultation for Apple TV+, Apple Music, and Netflix.

Crain's Detroit Business named Niles to its 20 in their 20s list in 2015 and its 40 Under 40 list in 2025.
