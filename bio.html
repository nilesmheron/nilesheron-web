Niles Heron

Entrepreneur, co-founder of Loaded, and Senior Director of Tech-Based Entrepreneurship at TechTown Detroit

bio

Niles Heron is a son of Detroit's Eastside who has spent twenty years doing one thing in a lot of different rooms: using technology, business, and whatever he's learned along the way to help people move forward. He has built companies, worked inside corporations, driven change, failed, and flown. Through all of it, the work has felt less like a career than a calling: serve the people around him, and leave the world a little better than he found it.

It started early. He took everything apart as a kid to see how it worked, built his first computer as a teenager at U of D Jesuit, and found in gaming something his neighborhood couldn't offer: a doorway to the world beyond Detroit. Before he turned 20, he was working with esports tournament organizers and top teams, helping send gamers around the globe to chase their dreams.

At 20 he moved to Los Angeles to pursue creative expression and opportunity. He found a seven-year career there in environmental health and safety, most of it at Amgen, which later took him to San Francisco. Those years taught him how large systems really work and how to change them from the inside. Then he came home. In 2014 he co-founded Michigan Funders, one of the state's first equity crowdfunding platforms, built to serve the people and businesses the markets forgot. It set the state record at the time when it helped Detroit City FC raise the money to rebuild Keyworth Stadium. Other ventures followed, a few exits and a few exciting failures, and each one taught him something he would later give away.

In 2016 he co-founded Loaded, which grew into the leading talent management and brand consulting business in gaming. Loaded represented creators like Ninja, TimTheTatman, DrLupo, Courage, and Shroud, and partnered with brands from Adidas to Epic Games to Red Bull. During his time there, Loaded raised $30M and generated upwards of $500M in gross revenue. Its success also gave him a way to open the door wider: he helped launch the Loaded Content Accelerator to back creators from marginalized communities.

Today it's the whole job. As Senior Director of Tech-Based Entrepreneurship at TechTown Detroit, he leads Venture 313, the Mobility Accelerator Innovation Network (MAIN), Gateway, and TechTown's Core Programs, which together represent upwards of $30M of philanthropic commitment across the programs. That work builds on a decade of teaching and mentoring through Techstars, gener8tor, and Pharrell Williams' Black Ambition, and more than 150 founders he has supported directly.

He still moonlights in livestreaming and entertainment, developing content and events for Revolt, 3BD, and DreamCon and consulting on marketing strategy for Apple TV+, Apple Music, and Netflix. Crain's Detroit Business has named him to its 20 in their 20s (2015) and 40 Under 40 (2025) lists.

Niles has been blessed to chase his dreams well beyond this city. The ones he holds most dearly, he's found, are here at home.
