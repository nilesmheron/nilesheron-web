Base annual cost is the line's normative yearly value. Each year's % modifier adjusts it. A modifier of 0 means "same as base"; −100% means "nothing that year"; +10% means "10% more than base" (or than prior year, if compound).

Compound: Y2 applies on top of Y1's dollar value, Y3 applies on top of Y2's. Good for salaries (3%/yr raises) or anything growing steadily. Off (independent): each year's modifier applies directly to the base. Good for partial-year starts, one-time costs, or anything that doesn't compound.

All fields are editable. Click name, note, base, or modifier to edit. Click × to delete a line. Use "Add line" below the table.

Scale badges (e.g. ×50% (preset)) indicate items running at a partial rate in the current scenario. Switch presets to clear them.

Burden and fees. Lines marked (W-2) are shown loaded with the W-2 burden rate in the header. The program lead line carries no allowance by default (the fee is the lead's, whole; comp terms are a separate document). The DFC fiduciary fee is computed on all selected lines and shown as its own row; by default Song carries it above the commitment and it is excluded from totals. Untick the box to fold it in. Rates are placeholders until confirmed.

Levels. Base is what has to work on Song Foundation's commitment alone. Mid adds the first staff and the full cohort programs on the first joint raise. Full is the staff complement, direct capital, GP back office, and a physical-home option reserve in Y3. Levels nest; each preset turns on the lines of the levels below it.

Source labels in notes follow the project convention: [YOURS] = Niles' stated position, [EXTRAPOLATION] = projected from his logic and open to confirmation, [GAP] = no position yet.
