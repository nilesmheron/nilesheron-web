short bio

A proud product of Detroit's Eastside, Niles Heron has spent twenty years using technology and entrepreneurship to help people move forward. He co-founded Loaded, gaming's leading talent firm, and spent seven years growing it to $30M raised and upwards of $500M in revenue while launching an accelerator for marginalized creators. Today he leads Tech-Based Entrepreneurship at TechTown Detroit, stewarding upwards of $30M in philanthropic commitment. He is a Crain's 20 in their 20s and 40 Under 40 honoree.
