Niles Heron

Senior Director of Tech-Based Entrepreneurship, TechTown Detroit

short bio, 75 words

A proud product of the Eastside, Niles Heron is Senior Director of Tech-Based Entrepreneurship at TechTown Detroit, leading Venture 313, the Mobility Accelerator Innovation Network, Gateway, and TechTown's Core Programs, representing over $30 million in philanthropic commitment. He previously helped guide gaming and livestreaming firm Loaded to $30 million in equity investment and nearly $500 million in revenue. He is a Crain's Detroit Business 20 in their 20s (2015) and 40 Under 40 (2025) honoree.
