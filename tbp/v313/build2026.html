Detroit Homecoming Week · September 2026
VENTURE 313 BUILDATHON

Detroit builds its
own solutions.

This September, builders, dreamers, and product developers from across Detroit come together for five days to design and ship digital tools that address real municipal challenges. Hosted by Venture 313 — the Gilbert Family Foundation‑backed initiative supporting Detroit tech founders — in partnership with the City of Detroit, the Buildathon anchors Detroit Homecoming week as its opening act.

How It Works
01 · AUG 20
Applications open
Citywide call for builders, with coordinated announcement and comms push
02 · SEP 17–18
Orient & train
Registration, team formation, and hands-on AI‑assisted development education
03 · SEP 19–21
Build weekend
Teams build working digital solutions to their chosen challenge
04 · SEP 22
Showcase
Public presentations, winners announced, awards presented

Participants receive training in the latest AI-assisted development techniques — working with tools like Claude Code, Lovable, ChatGPT, GitHub, and Vercel. No prior software experience is required; the education is built in. Teams then build through the weekend and present finished products at the September 22 Showcase.

The Track Record

This is the third Buildathon. The first two produced companies, careers, and builders who stayed in the ecosystem.

Y2

313SafeBeds goes live

ShaTera launched 313SafeBeds — a product born at the Buildathon and carried forward beyond it.

Y2

Drone companies, live first-responder showcases

Rajesh and Everette each built drone companies and staged live first-responder technology demonstrations. Rajesh is still building at TechTown today — developing his software and competing for public and private contracts.

Y2

Non-technical students learn to build

A team of non-technical Wayne State students learned to code during the event and built a community app addressing food scarcity — neighbors taking care of neighbors — narrowly missing third place.

The path is non-linear — and the wins compound.

Pouring into people gives them the context to stay in the ecosystem, and the indirect winners keep surfacing: Tricee Clark (Buildathon Y1) went on to win Hatch. Aaron is now a full-time engineer. ADA graduates who competed in Y2 are now build engineers at TechTown’s Build 313 nights today.
