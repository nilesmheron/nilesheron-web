How the renewal was assembled — where the money moved

Start Studio: MVP’s $300,000 is preserved and split three ways, seeding $70,000 into the new IV Fund. The TSF Community Fund contributes $100,000 to that same fund; its remaining $50,000 was not renewed — the cycle’s full reduction.

The build-tier reduction is possible because the cost of building software has fallen sharply. Smaller, well-placed subsidies now reach more founders than a single concentrated program did.

“IV” carries a deliberate double meaning: it marks Year IV of this vehicle’s evolution from the original Community Fund, and it signals an intravenous injection of new resource directly into the founder ecosystem. Prior-cycle counts are not additive with current targets (the ~25 TSF figure is cumulative over three years), so reach is compared program-to-program, not as a single total.
